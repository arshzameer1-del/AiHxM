# Competitor website notes (for positioning, not for copying)

Read on 2026-10-23 from each company's own homepage. Everything below is what *they* claim; none of it was verified.
The site does not name or compare against competitors (decision for this build).

| Site | What they lead with | Patterns worth noting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PayPeople | "AI Powered One Stop HR Solution", sign-up plus demo | AI agents, integrations wall, awards, customer videos, pricing page |
| StreamHCM | HR and payroll "your team will actually use" | Payroll "4 hours vs 3 days" claim, "22 modules and 300+ features", biometric brand list, customer stories |
| SoftHCM | "People, Payroll & Performance, one intelligent HRMS" | Rating and employee-count badge, industries, 7-step implementation, statutory compliance list |
| HTA HRMS | Low-cost modular HR and payroll, on-premise or cloud | "Affordable price", one-time licence, stat counters, client logos |
| AidenHR, eHR | Not usable: AidenHR page was not retrievable; the eHR address (ehr.com.pk) is an unrelated electronic-health-records site | |

What they share: dashboard mock-ups, large feature counts, speed claims ("minutes, not days"), client-logo strips, star ratings
and AI language, usually without evidence on the page.

Where AiHxM's site deliberately differs:

1. Every capability carries a status (Available, Needs your setup, Partly built, Not built yet) and gaps are listed.
2. A dedicated Pakistan payroll page with a table of what exists and which official formats are missing.
3. Real screenshots of the running product with sample data, labelled as such.
4. No invented statistics, logos, ratings, customer stories, certifications or prices.
5. Security page states the controls that can be checked and what is not claimed.
6. A short demo form with a real stored destination, a privacy notice and no tracking cookies.
