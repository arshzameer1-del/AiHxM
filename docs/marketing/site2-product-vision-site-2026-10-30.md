# SITE2: product-vision website, trust and commercial pages (2026-10-30)

## Direction (owner, 2026-10-30)
The site is built for the product AiHxM intends to sell commercially in about a year, not only for what is finished today.
Public pages present the full intended offering. Internal status labels (In development, Partially available, Planned, etc.)
are never shown publicly. Real readiness lives in `docs/internal/AIHXM_PRODUCT_LAUNCH_READINESS.md`, which is private and
regenerated by `node apps/site/scripts/readiness-doc.mjs`. Hard rules that stay: no invented customers, testimonials,
statistics, certifications, uptime figures, prices, support commitments, partner integrations or security assurances.

## What changed
- Public status chips, the status legend, module "Good to know" notes and limitation wording removed (flag `showStatusLabels` = false in `flags.mjs`). `check.mjs` now fails the build if internal status wording or "not built" style wording appears on any page, or if the readiness register lands in `dist`.
- New product pages: Benefits Management, Shift Management, Advanced Workforce Analytics, Intelligent HR (forward-looking), Business Travel, Announcements. Core HR page is now "Core HR & Employee Management". Platform now has 26 product areas.
- Trust Center (/trust/), Security matrix (/security/, three neutral states: in the product / set by your company / agreed per customer), buyer's checklist.
- Pricing structure (/pricing/): plan tiers, what is priced separately, implementation and migration cost drivers, hosting options, enterprise quotation steps. Scope estimator (/pricing/estimator/): shows plan structure and scope size, never a price. Price arithmetic exists (unit-tested with a synthetic config) and stays off until `approvedPrices` and `flags.pricingPublished` are set.
- Implementation (/implementation/): eight phases with AiHxM / customer responsibilities and deliverables; support tiers on /services/support/ (channels, hours, response targets are blank until approved).
- Pakistan payroll implementation (/pakistan-hrms/payroll-implementation/): nine workstreams split between AiHxM, the company and its accountant. No rates stated.
- Public playground (/playground/): browser-only simulation on fictional data (profile, attendance, leave, approval, payroll, payslip, analytics). No network, no storage, invented tax bands labelled as not Pakistan's.
- Forms: new "quote" type, scope prefill, `from` context added to every demo link and sent with the request. Case-study architecture (empty; publishes only entries with a recorded customer approval). Company facts and founder block render only from env vars (MARKETING_LEGAL_NAME, _REG_NO, _ADDRESS, _FOUNDER_NAME, _FOUNDER_ROLE, _FOUNDER_BIO).

- About page rebuilt from the owner's About Us document: 11 storytelling sections with sticky in-page navigation, real product screenshots (demonstration company), animated challenge and configuration comparisons, module explorer, AI direction map labelled as direction, Beyond HR ecosystem, founder section (Kumail Sherazi; monogram until a photo is added at `apps/site/src/assets/brand/founder.jpg`), mission/vision/values and a five-step timeline. AI, ERP, agents and automation are labelled as direction; no affiliation with any software vendor is implied.
